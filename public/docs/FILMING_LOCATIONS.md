### Boulder / Front Range
**2,153 clips · 5,026–10,187 ft · Boulder, CO**

The most-filmed area by a wide margin — over half the entire library. Well-covered
at foothills elevation (5,000–8,000 ft). The high alpine zone (10,000+ ft) around
Brainard Lake and the Indian Peaks is largely uncovered despite being 45 min away.

**What's well covered:**
| Location | Clips | Elevation | Scene Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flagstaff Mountain | 415 | 5,312–6,890 ft | Rocky foothills, open views |
| Valmont Butte | 335 | 5,200–5,305 ft | Open prairie, reservoir |
| Shanahan Hill | 260 | 5,397–5,978 ft | Scrubby foothills |
| The Flatirons | 207 | 5,636–7,090 ft | Iconic sandstone slabs |
| Mount Sanitas | 194 | 5,322–6,060 ft | Open ridgeline |
| Table Mountain | 125 | 5,302–6,296 ft | Mesa top, urban fringe |
| Cannon Mountain | 74 | 8,094–8,383 ft | Subalpine, dense forest |
| Miller Rock | 77 | 8,586–8,638 ft | Rocky subalpine |
| Overland Mountain | 25 | 8,366–8,432 ft | Open subalpine |

**Coverage gaps within 1 hour of home:**
- Bald Mountain (9,127 ft) — Magnolia Rd access, no coverage
- Sugarloaf Mountain (8,887 ft) — Sugarloaf Rd, no coverage
- Burnt Mountain (9,251 ft) — Gold Hill area, no coverage
- South Boulder Peak / Bear Peak (8,500 ft) — OSMP, no coverage
- All peaks above 9,500 ft — zero coverage (see `HIKE_TARGETS_SNOW.md`)

**Base:** Boulder, CO. No camping needed for day shoots.

### Rocky Mountain National Park
**145 clips · 7,484–10,810 ft · Estes Park, CO · 1.5h from Boulder**

Coverage exists but is almost entirely road-level: Trail Ridge Road, Bear Lake Road,
Sprague Lake Road. The actual backcountry — alpine lakes, tundra above 11,000 ft,
the Loch Vale drainage — has not been filmed.

**What's been shot:**
- Trail Ridge Road corridor (tundra overlooks from the car, 27 clips)
- Bear Lake / Sprague Lake / Nymph Lake area (25 clips, lakeside)
- Estes Park town area and lower valley

**Priority gaps:**
- Dream Lake (10,153 ft) — stunning tundra lake, 3 mi from Bear Lake TH
- Emerald Lake (10,110 ft) — waterfall feed, 3.6 mi from Bear Lake TH
- Lake Haiyaha — less-visited, excellent rock and water
- Wild Basin: Calypso Cascades and Ouzel Falls — uncovered, south end of park
- Tundra above Trail Ridge (12,000+ ft) — short walk from road, zero coverage
- Twin Sisters Peaks (11,332 ft) — zero coverage, great sunrise location

**Camping:** Moraine Park Campground (reserve on recreation.gov).
Wild Basin Campground for south-end shoots.

**Best timing:** July 4–25 for tundra wildflowers; September for golden aspen
in the lower valleys.

### Indian Peaks Wilderness
**173 clips · 8,045–10,623 ft · Nederland / Ward, CO · 1h from Boulder**

Mostly Parsenn Bowl (116 clips, ski-area access) and Devils Thumb Trail.
The high alpine lake chain — Long Lake, Lake Isabelle, Pawnee Lake — is uncovered.

**What's been shot:**
- Parsenn Bowl ski area terrain (116 clips — likely shoulder season snow/slush)
- Devils Thumb Trail lower section (24 clips)
- Rainbow Lakes Road pull-offs (19 clips)
- Pomeroy Mountain approach (19 clips)

**Priority gaps — all reachable from Brainard Lake TH:**
- Lake Isabelle (10,868 ft) — iconic Indian Peaks lake, zero coverage
- Long Lake (10,460 ft) — 5 min from parking, zero coverage
- Mount Audubon summit (13,208 ft) — above treeline tundra, 8 mi RT
- Niwot Ridge — high alpine research area, open tundra
- Pawnee Pass trail upper section and Pawnee Lake

**Access:** County Rd 102 from Ward. Road typically opens early May.
Parking requires a timed-entry reservation July–Sep on weekends.

**Camping:** Pawnee Campground (near Brainard Lake), or camp at
Peaceful Valley Campground lower down.

### Gunnison / Crested Butte
**578 clips · 6,490–10,525 ft · Mount Crested Butte, CO · 4h from Boulder**

Second-largest area in the library. Well-covered around Ruby Peak (Green Lake Road),
Crested Butte town and ski area, and the Lake Irwin / Kebler Pass road. Maroon Bells
is counted here geographically (the road originates near Crested Butte).

**What's been shot:**
| Location | Clips | Elevation | Access Road |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ruby Peak / Green Lake Road | 206 | 8,862–10,525 ft | Kebler Pass Rd corridor |
| Lake Irwin Road | 40 | 10,318–10,390 ft | Off Kebler Pass Rd |
| Marcellina Mountain corridor | 13 | 8,389–9,094 ft | Kebler Pass Rd (CO-12) |
| Crested Butte / ski area | 136 | 8,865–10,364 ft | Peakview Drive |
| Ohio Peak / Ohio Creek Road | 6 | 9,180–9,869 ft | Ohio Creek Rd south |
| Mount Emmons | 3 | ~9,088 ft | Near Crested Butte |
| **Kebler Pass corridor total** | **~220** | **8,389–10,525 ft** | Green Lake Rd + Lake Irwin |

**Gaps within this area:**
- **Peak aspen timing** — the Kebler corridor is well-covered but likely shot
  outside peak color; return September 18–22 specifically for that window
- **Ohio Creek Road** — only 6 clips; the valley south of Ruby Peak in peak color
  is outstanding and is the biggest remaining gap close to existing coverage
- East River drainage — Crested Butte to Gothic road, wildflowers in July; zero coverage
- Cement Creek valley — parallel drainage, zero coverage
- Mount Crested Butte summit area above the ski lifts

**Camping:** Lake Irwin Campground (10,161 ft, opens mid-June, very popular).
Gothic Campground near the RMBL research station.
Cement Creek Campground for quieter option.

**Best timing:** July 10–25 for wildflowers (Gothic/East River meadows are
exceptional). Late September for Kebler Pass aspen.

### Maroon Bells / Aspen
**208 clips · 8,058–10,131 ft · Aspen, CO · 3.5h from Boulder**

Coverage is almost entirely from Maroon Lake Road and lower Maroon Creek Road —
essentially the viewpoint area at the lake. The upper valley and the wilderness
above the bells has not been visited.

**What's been shot:**
- Maroon Lake Road pull-offs and lake viewpoints (87 clips)
- Maroon Creek Road lower section (15 clips)
- Sievers Mountain approach (122 clips — likely from Crested Butte side)
- Pyramid Peak viewpoints (25 clips)

**Priority gaps:**
- Crater Lake (10,076 ft) — 3.4 mi from Maroon Lake TH, above treeline
- West Maroon Creek trail upper section
- Buckskin Pass approach
- Aspen grove along lower Maroon Creek — early morning mist shots

**Access note:** Maroon Lake Road is gated; mandatory shuttle from Aspen
Highlands parking area late June–early October. Arrive by 7 AM to drive in,
otherwise take the shuttle.

**Camping:** Maroon Lake Campground (extremely competitive, book 6 months out)
or Silver Bell Campground lower down on Maroon Creek Rd.

**Best timing:** July 15–August 1 for wildflowers. First week of October for
aspen color (one of Colorado's most photographed spots in fall).

### Collegiate Peaks / Buena Vista
**143 clips · 7,303–12,126 ft · Buena Vista, CO · 2.5h from Boulder**

Concentrated around Chalk Creek Drive (Nathrop area, 32 clips) and Tigger Peak /
Midland Hill (68 clips combined). The 14ers (Yale, Harvard, Columbia, Oxford) and
their approach trails have almost no coverage.

**What's been shot:**
- Chalk Creek Drive / St. Elmo ghost town corridor (32 clips)
- Tigger Peak area (41 clips, ~9,000 ft)
- Midland Hill (27 clips, Buena Vista outskirts)
- Bald Mountain / Sheep Mountain in the valley (48 clips)

**Priority gaps:**
- Hartman Rocks Recreation Area (Gunnison side)
- Arkansas River through Buena Vista — river footage, rafting corridor
- Cottonwood Lake (10,086 ft) — near St. Elmo, easy access
- Browns Canyon National Monument — dramatic river canyon, BLM land
- Mount Princeton Hot Springs area — meadows + peak backdrop

**Camping:** Chalk Lake Campground (near St. Elmo), Mount Princeton area
campgrounds, Browns Canyon area BLM sites.

### Curecanti NRA / Blue Mesa Reservoir
**16 clips · 7,510–9,199 ft · Gunnison, CO · 3.5h from Boulder**

Very sparse. Only Curecanti Needle area (4 clips), Sapinero Point (3 clips),
Castle View Point (7 clips). This is on the road between Gunnison and Montrose
and is likely incidental footage from the Crested Butte trips.

**What's here that's uncovered:**
- Blue Mesa Reservoir — Colorado's largest body of water, with dramatic
  canyon walls dropping to a deep blue lake. Very loopable.
- Morrow Point Reservoir — only accessible by boat or trail, extremely scenic
  Gunnison River canyon
- Pioneer Point overlook — sweeping views over the canyon system

This is also the gateway to Black Canyon of the Gunnison NP (30 min west).

### Steamboat Springs Area
**9 clips · 6,900–7,589 ft · Steamboat Springs, CO · 3.5h from Boulder**

Essentially zero coverage. Only 9 clips, all apparently incidental. The Yampa
Valley and Mount Zirkel Wilderness are completely unrepresented.

**What's worth filming here:**
- Steamboat Lake SP — alpine lake at 8,000 ft with Hahns Peak backdrop (2,500 ft
  extinct volcano)
- Pearl Lake SP — quieter, excellent reflections
- Mount Zirkel Wilderness — Big Creek Lakes, Lakes of the Clouds
- Strawberry Park Hot Springs area
- Yampa River corridor through town

**Camping:** Steamboat Lake SP (lakeside sites, reserve April–August).

### Texas Hill Country

cedar/live oak, and Hill Country limestone.

**What's been shot:**

### Silverton / San Juan Mountains ⭐ Priority Trip
**0 clips — Planned for summer 2026 · 5.5h from Boulder**

The San Juan Mountains are the most dramatic range in Colorado and the
single biggest gap in this library. Silverton sits at 9,318 ft in the
Animas River valley, surrounded by 13,000–14,000 ft peaks.

**Best timing for green + wildflowers: July 10–25**
- Wildflowers peak at alpine elevations (10,000–12,500 ft)
- Snow still visible on 13,000+ ft peaks — green meadow to snow contrast
- All passes open; Ophir/Cinnamon/Engineer accessible
- Aspens still green (haven't turned)
- Late June: higher water in Animas, some passes still opening

**Key filming locations:**

| Location | Distance from Silverton | Elevation | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Molas Lake | 6 mi south on US 550 | 9,840 ft | **Must-shoot** |
| Ice Lake Basin | 5 mi (3 mi hike) via South Mineral Campground | 12,257 ft | **Must-shoot** |
| Coal Bank Pass pullouts | 12 mi south on US 550 | 10,640 ft | Easy roadside |
| Ophir Pass | 8 mi west via CR 8 (4WD) | 11,789 ft | High-alpine meadows |
| Cunningham Gulch | 4 mi east of Silverton | 10,400 ft | Old mines + tundra |
| Animas River corridor | In town | 9,318 ft | River footage |
| Engineer Pass | 20 mi east (4WD, connects to Lake City) | 12,800 ft | Extreme alpine |
| Cinnamon Pass | 20 mi east (4WD) | 12,620 ft | High alpine basin |
| Durango–Silverton Railway | Silverton depot | 9,318 ft | Iconic steam train |

**Molas Lake** — 6 miles south of Silverton on US 550. Roadside pull-off with
a short walk to the lake (9,840 ft). Perfect reflections of the Grenadier Range.
One of the most iconic viewpoints in the San Juans and completely uncovered.

**Ice Lake Basin** — Turquoise alpine lake (12,257 ft) surrounded by wildflower
fields and 13,000 ft peaks. 3-mile hike from South Mineral Campground. Access via
FR 585, 5 miles north of Silverton off US 550. July 10–25 is peak wildflower timing.
This is genuinely one of the most beautiful alpine basins in Colorado.

**Camping base:** South Mineral Campground (9,500 ft, 5 mi from Silverton on FR 585).
Directly on the Animas River. Puts you at the Ice Lake trailhead and the Bandora Mine
road. Usually opens mid-June. Book on recreation.gov — fills every weekend in July.

**Alternate base:** Molas Lake Campground (10,500 ft) — right at the lake, but
fills instantly in July. Or dispersed BLM camping in Cunningham Gulch (free, no
reservation, fire restrictions may apply).

**Access routes:**
- US 550 (Million Dollar Highway) from Ouray — open year-round but narrow and
  dramatic with no guardrails on the canyon side
- US 550 from Durango — gentler approach from the south, passes Molas Lake
- 4WD routes (Ophir, Engineer, Cinnamon) open by late June; check
  [sanjuancountycolorado.us](https://www.sanjuancountycolorado.us) for pass status

**What to film in the Animas Canyon:**
The Durango & Silverton Narrow Gauge Railroad runs through the Animas Canyon
(inaccessible by road). The train itself can be ridden from Durango, but
**Elk Park** stop (accessible by train only) puts you in the heart of the
Weminuche Wilderness with no other way in short of a multi-day backpack. If
planning a backcountry extension, Elk Park is the entry point.

**Nearby on the way back (stopping points):**
- Ouray (2h north of Silverton) — Box Canyon Falls, Ouray Ice Park area,
  amphitheater canyon
- Ridgway SP — lakeside with stunning Cimarron Range backdrop
- Dallas Divide overlook on CO-62 — famous Sneffels Range view

### Great Sand Dunes NP
**0 clips — Recommended next camping trip · 4h from Boulder**

Completely unique content type. No other location in the library has sand
dunes. Medano Creek flows across the dune base from April through early July —
a shallow, clear stream over sand, excellent looping subject.

**Best timing: May–early June** (Medano Creek at peak flow).
July is busy; creek often stops by late July.

**Key filming locations:**
- Medano Creek crossing at dune base (May–June only)
- Main dune field at sunrise (arrive before 6 AM for solitude)
- Zapata Falls — 5 min drive south of park entrance, 30 ft waterfall in a slot
- Sangre de Cristo peaks above the dunes (San Isabel NF)
- Star Dune summit (750 ft above valley floor)

**Camping:** Piñon Flats Campground inside the park. Reserve 6 months out
for May/June weekends. Backup: Zapata Falls Campground (BLM, south of park).

**Drive:** Boulder → I-25 S → Walsenburg → CO-160 W → CO-150 N.
No mountain passes. ~4 hours.

### Black Canyon of the Gunnison NP
**0 clips — Natural add-on to Crested Butte trips · 3.5h from Boulder**

40 minutes west of Gunnison. The canyon is 2,722 ft deep with near-vertical
Precambrian schist walls. The Gunnison River at the bottom is accessible via
East Portal Road (drops 2,000 ft in 4 miles — do not attempt with a trailer).

**Key filming locations:**
- South Rim overlooks: Tomichi Point, Gunnison Point, Chasm View
- East Portal: Gunnison River level — rushing whitewater, crystal clear water
- Painted Wall overlook — tallest cliff face in Colorado (2,250 ft)
- Warner Point — 1.5 mi hike, best full-canyon views

**Camping:** South Rim Campground (no reservation needed, first-come).
East Portal Campground is 2 sites right at the river — fill very fast.

### Pikes Peak / South Park Area
**0 clips · 2.5h from Boulder**

The closest uncovered major Colorado landmark. Accessible without camping
as a long day trip, or overnight in the Woodland Park / Eleven Mile area.

**Key filming locations:**
- Pikes Peak summit road (14,115 ft) — above-treeline tundra, alpine lakes
- Crystal Creek Reservoir on the way up
- Eleven Mile Canyon — South Platte River through a granite canyon, excellent
  water footage
- Mueller SP — dense ponderosa pine, meadows, Pikes Peak views
- Horsethief Falls near Cripple Creek

### White River NF — Rifle Falls Area
**0 clips · 3.5h from Boulder**

Rifle Falls SP has a triple waterfall over travertine terraces — a unique
geological feature not represented anywhere else in the library. The cave
system behind the falls adds a completely different visual element.

Nearby: Rifle Gap Reservoir, and further west, the Canyon Pintado
petroglyphs corridor.

**Camping:** Rifle Falls SP campground, right at the falls.
