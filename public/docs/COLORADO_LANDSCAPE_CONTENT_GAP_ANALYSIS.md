## RISK MITIGATION

### Weather Risk:
- Problem: Mountain weather is unpredictable
- Solution: Plan shoots for reliable windows (e.g., July for peaks, Sept for migration)
- Backup: Spring shoots can shift to June if needed

### Wildlife Risk:
- Problem: Animal encounters (elk rut, bear season)
- Solution: Scout locations beforehand, travel with friend during risky times
- Backup: Use previously shot footage if safety requires delay

### Time Risk:
- Problem: 65–70 days is ambitious with day job
- Solution: Focus on weekends + strategic long weekends
- Prioritize highest-revenue opportunities (spring, fall, storms)

### Equipment Risk:
- Problem: Camera failures in harsh weather
- Solution: Maintain backup GoPro, test in conditions before critical shoots
- Weather-sealing and protective cases essential
